CHANDIGARH UNIVERSITY ADMISSIONS 2026-27

ADMISSION PROCESS:
Step 1: Register for CUCET (CU Common Entrance Test) — free registration
Step 2: Appear for CUCET (scholarship eligibility test)
Step 3: Receive CUCET score and scholarship offer
Step 4: Submit admission application and documents
Step 5: Pay fees and confirm enrollment

CUCET (CU Common Entrance Test):
- Purpose: Scholarship eligibility + merit evaluation
- Free to register
- Conducted online and at CU campus
- Phase II closes: 31st July 2026
- Results used for scholarship award up to 100% fee waiver

IMPORTANT DATES (2026-27):
- CUCET Phase II Last Date: 31st July 2026
- Orientation Week: 23rd July to 29th July 2026
- Academic Session Start: August 2026

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR ADMISSION:
- 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates
- Transfer Certificate (TC) from last institution
- Migration Certificate (if applicable)
- Character Certificate
- Medical Fitness Certificate
- 6 passport-size photographs
- Aadhar Card / Passport / Govt. ID proof
- Domicile Certificate (if applicable)
- JEE/NEET/CAT/CUET scores (if applying for merit scholarship)
