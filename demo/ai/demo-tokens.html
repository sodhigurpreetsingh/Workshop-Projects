💡 Key insights
  • 1
    1 token ≈ 4 chars in English — GPT models were trained mostly on English, so English text is the most efficient to process.
  • 2
    Code is efficient — keywords like def, return, if are often single tokens. Python is well-represented in training data.
  • 3
    Non-English scripts cost more — Hindi and Chinese characters split into more tokens per word. The same idea expressed in Hindi may use 2–3× as many tokens as in English.
  • 4
    Context limits are in tokens, not words — GPT-4o's 128K context window means ~128,000 tokens, roughly 96,000 English words — but far fewer Hindi words or lines of code.
