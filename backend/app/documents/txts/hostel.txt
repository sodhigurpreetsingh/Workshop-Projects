CHANDIGARH UNIVERSITY HOSTEL FACILITIES

ACCOMMODATION TYPES AND ANNUAL FEES:

AC ROOMS (Annual Fee per student):
- 5-Seater (Boys only): Rs 1,50,000 per year
- 4-Seater (Girls only): Rs 1,65,000 per year
- 3-Seater (Boys and Girls): Rs 1,80,000 per year
- 2-Seater (Boys only): Rs 2,08,000 per year
- 1-Seater/Single Room (Boys only): Rs 2,20,000 per year

NON-AC ROOMS (Annual Fee per student):
- 5-Seater (Boys and Girls): Rs 1,20,000 per year
- 4-Seater (Boys and Girls): Rs 1,35,000 per year
- 3-Seater (Boys and Girls): Rs 1,50,000 per year
- 2-Seater (Boys only): Rs 1,80,000 per year
- 1-Seater/Single Room (Boys only): Rs 1,95,000 per year

Note: Fee increases by Rs 2,000 per year due to food inflation adjustments.
All fees include meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner) from attached mess.

HOSTEL FACILITIES:
- 24×7 power supply with backup generators
- High-speed Wi-Fi internet in all rooms and common areas
- Fully furnished rooms (bed, wardrobe, study table, chair)
- Furnished common rooms for relaxation
- Gymnasium and fitness center
- Reading rooms and study halls
- Night canteen and tuck shops (open late hours)
- Salon/barber services on campus
- Visitor and guest rooms
- Sports courts: volleyball, basketball, badminton
- Music room with TV and cable access
- Medical facilities on campus

SECURITY AND SAFETY:
- 24×7 CCTV surveillance throughout hostel premises
- Restricted campus access with entry/exit logs
- Completely separate hostels for male and female students
- Dedicated wardens and support staff for each hostel block
- Strict entry/exit timings for safety

FOOD AND MESS:
- Attached mess with all three meals included in hostel fee
- Fresh and hygienic food prepared daily
- Balanced diet menu with special dietary options
- Separate mess for vegetarian and non-vegetarian food

HOSTEL RULES:
- Weapons and firearms strictly prohibited
- Alcohol, narcotics, and smoking strictly forbidden
- Visitors cannot bring intoxicants onto hostel premises
- Respectful behavior towards all residents and staff required
- Entry/exit timings must be followed
