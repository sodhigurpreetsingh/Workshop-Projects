Demo Institute of Technology - Leadership & Administration

DIRECTOR:
The Director of Demo Institute of Technology is Gurpreet Singh Sodhi.
Gurpreet Singh Sodhi oversees academic affairs, institutional development, industry partnerships, and the overall strategic direction of DIT. Students can reach the Director's office through the administration block or via the institute's official email.

ADMINISTRATION:
- Director: Gurpreet Singh Sodhi
- Academic administration, faculty affairs, and student grievances are handled through the Director's office
- The institute maintains an open-door policy — students with concerns are encouraged to approach faculty advisors or the administration office

VISION:
To be a centre of excellence in technical and management education, producing industry-ready graduates who contribute meaningfully to society.

MISSION:
Providing quality education through innovative teaching, research, and strong industry collaboration while nurturing ethical values and professional skills in students.
