CHANDIGARH UNIVERSITY SCHOLARSHIPS 2026-27

Total Scholarship Pool: Rs 200 Crore

1. CUCET SCHOLARSHIP (CU Common Entrance Test):
- Up to 100% fee waiver based on CUCET performance
- Renewal: Minimum 7.5 CGPA required every semester

2. JEE MAINS SCHOLARSHIP (for Engineering):
- AIR up to 25,000: 100% semester fee waiver
- AIR 25,001–50,000: 75% fee waiver
- AIR 50,001–75,000: 50% fee waiver
- AIR 75,001–1,00,000: 20% fee waiver
- AIR 1,00,001–1,25,000: 10% fee waiver

3. CLASS 12 MERIT SCHOLARSHIP (UG Programs):
- 90% and above marks: 50% fee waiver
- 85%–89.99% marks: 20% fee waiver
- 80%–84.99% marks: 10% fee waiver

4. CUET (NTA) SCHOLARSHIP:
- 90% percentile and above: 50% fee waiver
- 85%–89.99% percentile: 30% fee waiver
- 80%–84.99% percentile: 15% fee waiver
- 70%–79.99% percentile: 10% fee waiver

5. CAT SCHOLARSHIP (for MBA):
- 80% score and above: 50% fee waiver
- 75%–79.99%: 25% fee waiver
- 70%–74.99%: 10% fee waiver

6. GATE FELLOWSHIP (for ME programs):
- Rs 12,500 per month
- 8 months per year
- Requires 8–10 hours/week of teaching assistance

7. CGPA-BASED SCHOLARSHIP (Existing Students):
- 9.0 CGPA and above: 10% fee waiver per semester
- Applicable if no other active scholarship

8. SPORTS SCHOLARSHIP:
- Available for national and international level sports achievers
- Varying amounts based on achievement level
- Contact sports department for specific details

9. CULTURAL ACHIEVERS SCHOLARSHIP:
- National/International 1st place: 100% fee + free accommodation + free meals
- National 2nd/3rd: 75% fee + free accommodation + free meals
- State level winner: 50% fee waiver
- State participant: 20–30% fee waiver

10. SPECIAL CATEGORY SCHOLARSHIPS:
- Single girl child: 20% fee waiver
- Defence personnel wards: 20% fee concession (5% reserved seats)
- Kargil martyr wards: 15% fee waiver
- Orphan candidates: 50% fee waiver
- Single parent: 20% fee waiver
- CU/CGC alumni ward: 10% fee waiver
- Sibling studying at CU: 10% fee waiver

11. KALPANA CHAWLA SCHOLARSHIP (Girls from Chandigarh EWS):
- Eligibility: Girls from Chandigarh, family income below Rs 3 lakh/year
- Amount: 10% academic fee waiver
- 10 seats per year (first-come-first-served)

12. LADAKH MERITORIOUS STUDENTS:
- 80–85% in 12th (CBSE): 25% waiver
- 85.1–90%: 35% waiver
- Above 90%: 50% waiver
- Maximum 25 seats per year

SCHOLARSHIP RENEWAL CONDITIONS:
- Most scholarships require minimum 7.5 CGPA
- No E/F/I grades in regular semester exams
- Good conduct and attendance requirements apply
